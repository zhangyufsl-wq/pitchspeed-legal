PitchSpeed Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

PitchSpeed is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not run servers that receive your videos, measurements or player names.

What stays on your device

  • Videos you record or import are processed locally to measure speed and are not uploaded.
  • Player names and pitch measurements are stored only on your device.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to verify purchase status; it receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts, not your name, videos or measurements. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Camera and Photos

The camera is used only to record pitch videos. Photo library access is used only when you choose to import a video.

Children

PitchSpeed is intended for use by parents and coaches. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
