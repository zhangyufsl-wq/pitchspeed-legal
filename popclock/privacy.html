PopClock Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

PopClock is an iOS app that times baseball and softball plays (catcher pop time, home to first, 60-yard dash) from slow-motion video. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: everything stays on your phone.

Videos and measurements

Videos you record or import, the frames you mark, the times calculated from them, player names and exported clips are processed and stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Deleting a measurement in the app deletes its saved video. Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

Camera and photo library

The camera is used only to record slow-motion video when you tap record. Photo library access is used only to import a video you choose and to save clips or result cards you choose to export. PopClock does not scan or upload your library.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. To unlock PopClock Pro across your devices we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and your purchase history from Apple. This is used only to confirm your purchase and is not linked to your name, email or any other personal information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not collect

No account, no email, no location, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.

Children

PopClock does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Player names you enter are stored only on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
