Getting accurate times

  • Record inside PopClock at 240 fps, or import an iPhone Slo-mo video (allow full Photos access so PopClock can read the original high-frame-rate file).
  • Keep both ends of the play in frame — mitt and second base, plate and first base, or start and finish line — and hold the phone still.
  • Mark the first frame of each event: the first frame the ball touches the mitt, the first frame it has left the hand, the first frame it reaches the glove.
  • Pop time is timed mitt-to-glove, the same way scouts time it. The throw speed uses the standard home-to-second distance (127 ft 3 in baseball, 84 ft 10 in softball) unless you enter a custom one.

Sharing clips

On the result screen, tap Share clip with time overlay. PopClock trims the video around your marks, slows pop-time clips to half speed, and renders the time, label and player name into the video. Clips are rendered on your phone and shared through the normal iOS share sheet.
