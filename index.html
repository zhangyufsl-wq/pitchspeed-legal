Getting accurate readings

  • Film from the side, roughly halfway between the mound and home plate.
  • Keep both the pitcher and the catcher in frame and hold the phone still.
  • Choose the correct level so the pitching distance is right.
  • Mark the first frame the ball is out of the hand, and the first frame it touches the glove.
